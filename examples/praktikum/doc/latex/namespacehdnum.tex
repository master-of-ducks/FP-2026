Compute orthogonality and representation errors in FP1024 precision. 

The function converts {\ttfamily A\+\_\+low} and {\ttfamily Q\+\_\+low} to {\ttfamily FP1024} and computes two metrics used throughout the experiments:\+
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Orthogonality error:\+ Frobenius norm of {\ttfamily Q\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}T Q -\/ I}.
\item Representation error:\+ {\ttfamily \texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}A -\/ Q(\+Q\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}\+T A)\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\+\_\+F /\+ \texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\+A\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}\+\_\+F} (relative error).
\end{DoxyItemize}
